Arvo Player Privacy Policy

App: Arvo Player – Video & Audio
Android package: com.ashrafullah.arvoplayer
Developer: Ashrafullah

Effective date and last updated: 10 October 2026

1. About this policy

This policy explains how Arvo Player handles information when you play local media, use online content, view advertisements, or contact support. Local playback does not require an Arvo Player account. Online services and advertising providers receive information as described below.

2. Local media and app preferences

Arvo Player accesses audio, video, subtitles, and folders that you select or make accessible to the app. It uses file names, paths, and media metadata to display your library, play files, and perform editing or export operations. These operations do not upload your local media to a developer-operated server.

The app stores preferences and playback information on your device, including library entries, playlists, favourites, recent items, playback positions, bookmarks, and display or playback settings. Media exports are saved to the destination selected through the app.

If you choose to share a file using another app, the selected file is made available to that app. Its handling of the file is subject to that app's privacy practices.

3. Permissions and device features

File access is used to open and save media. Where requested by Android, notification permission supports playback controls. Background playback and wake-lock features help audio continue while the screen is off. Internet access supports online playback, web content, advertisements, and advertising privacy messages.

You can manage permissions in your device settings. Restricting a permission may limit the corresponding feature.

4. Advertising and Google services

Arvo Player integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements and Google's User Messaging Platform to manage advertising consent and privacy choices. Depending on your region, choices, device settings, and the advertising configuration, Google collects and shares:

  • IP address, which can be used to estimate approximate location.
  • App and advertising interactions, such as launches, taps, and views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information about the app and advertising SDK.
  • Device or other identifiers, including the advertising ID and app set ID, and applicable identifiers associated with accounts on the device.

Google uses this information for advertising, measurement and analytics, and fraud prevention. This collection can occur through the advertising SDK even though your media library remains on your device.

Where available, use the app's Ad privacy choices option to review your choices. You can also manage advertising identifiers in Android settings. These controls do not necessarily stop all technical data processing required for advertising or fraud prevention.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

5. YouTube, websites, and online media

When you use the built-in YouTube browser, searches and browsing requests are sent to YouTube and its service providers. Websites can receive your IP address, browser or device information, cookies, requested URLs, searches, and interactions. Their policies govern their processing.

When you open an online media address, your device connects to the relevant provider. The provider receives the address requested and connection information needed to deliver the content. Avoid opening private or sensitive links from untrusted sources.

Third-party services may process information in countries other than your own. Arvo Player does not control their retention practices or their account systems.

6. Content screening

The current Android release does not provide automatic frame-based content screening. Where a supported desktop version enables a local content detector, sampled frames are passed to a process on the device for analysis rather than uploaded by Arvo Player to a screening server. Screening may be unavailable or inaccurate and is not a guarantee that unsuitable content will be blocked.

7. Support communications

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information or attachments you choose to send. We use these to respond, investigate issues, and maintain relevant support records. Our email service provider also processes these communications.

Send only the information needed for your request. Bug reports may contain device identifiers, file paths, or other personal information; review them before sharing.

8. Information sharing

Information is disclosed to Google advertising and consent services, online content providers you access, apps you choose when sharing, and the email provider used for support, as described above. Support information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to investigate abuse or protect legal rights.

We do not sell your local media files or upload your local media library to a developer-operated advertising database.

9. Retention and deletion

Local app settings and playback records remain until removed through available app controls or by clearing app storage in Android settings. Uninstalling normally removes app-private storage. Device or cloud backups may retain copies according to your backup settings.

Original media and exported files saved outside app-private storage may remain after uninstalling. Delete those files separately using your file manager if you want to remove them.

Support communications are retained for as long as needed to address the request and related follow-up, or meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by contacting us. Any necessary exceptions will be explained in our response.

Google and other providers determine retention for data they receive. Clearing Arvo Player storage does not delete their server-side records. Use their privacy controls or contact them for applicable deletion requests.

10. Security

Local processing limits the need to transfer your media. Google states that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. The security of other online media connections depends on the provider and protocol; unencrypted HTTP or RTSP connections do not offer the protections of HTTPS or other encrypted protocols.

No device, storage system, or network connection can be guaranteed completely secure.

11. Children and third-party content

Online content may be unsuitable for children. This policy does not represent a guarantee of child-safe browsing. Parents and guardians should supervise access to third-party websites and media.

If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us through support, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

12. Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose which files to open, manage permissions, remove local app data, and use available advertising privacy controls. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict personal information we hold about you. Email us to make a request. We may need appropriate verification before acting.

We cannot retrieve your local library remotely or delete information held independently by another provider.

13. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the app's data practices change and revise the date above. Where required, significant changes will also be communicated through the app or another appropriate notice.
